# Wunder

> Wunder (formerly Wunder Capital; wewunder.com, now wunderpower.com) is a commercial clean-energy company that deploys solar, battery storage, and EV charging infrastructure across real estate portfolios in 40+ US states, offering no-CapEx energy solutions and managing projects from design through operations. Backed by Techstars, with a $650M partnership with Blackstone. Wunder publishes no public API or developer program; its Astra platform is an internal, proprietary system for energy data analysis, forecasting, and project automation.
